# Laptop Hardware Replacement and Troubleshooting

## Overview
Completed hands-on laptop hardware replacements, including RAM, battery, and a 2.5-inch SATA hard drive replacement with a SATA SSD.

## Hardware Work
- Removed and replaced laptop RAM modules.
- Removed and replaced the laptop battery.
- Removed the existing hard drive from its mounting caddy.
- Transferred the mounting caddy to the replacement SATA SSD.
- Installed the replacement drive and checked its connections.

## Troubleshooting
Encountered a boot issue during the hardware work. Checked component connections and reseated the RAM. The laptop subsequently booted successfully.

## Skills Practiced
- Identifying laptop components
- Removing and installing RAM
- Replacing a laptop battery
- Replacing a SATA storage drive
- Transferring a drive mounting caddy
- Checking connections and troubleshooting boot issues
